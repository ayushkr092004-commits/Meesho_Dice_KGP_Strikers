    How this plugs into the Valmo stack
    • Listens to the existing seller.order_ready event from order management. No seller-side change.
    • Holds fm.allocate until the customer confirms or the hub policy times out, so no linehaul is spent on uncommitted orders.
    • Writes the risk tier and intent status to the shipment record. The Pilot app reads it to set attempt priority and bonus eligibility (tab 2).
    • Channel adapters share one action set, so WhatsApp, SMS and IVR can be added or swapped per pincode without touching rules.

    Pay pilots for attempts that really happened

    A verified attempt needs the pilot inside the geofence plus proof: a doorway photo or a masked call. Bonuses stack by distance, zone and tier, with a per-shift cap so routes can't be farmed.
